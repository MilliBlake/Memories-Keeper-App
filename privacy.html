Privacy Policy

Memories Keeper is designed with privacy in mind. Memory data is stored on your device and may sync through Apple services, including iCloud, when available. If you create an optional Memories Keeper account, you can also sync memories across your devices and set up legacy delivery through our own service — end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read your memories. You control when data is exported or shared.

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Data We Collect

  • Memory content: memory text entries you create.
  • Photos and media: photos you attach to memories. If you enable "Auto-Save to Photos" in Settings, photos, videos, and voice memos captured inside the app are also saved to your device's Photo Library. This is optional, off by default, and the saved media never leaves your device or iCloud Photo Library.
  • Voice notes: audio recordings you attach to memories.
  • Location data: optional location attached to a memory when you choose to add it.
  • Contacts and relationships: legacy contact or shared-memory contact details only if you add or link them.
  • Account information (optional): if you create a Memories Keeper account with Sign in with Apple, we receive and store your Apple user identifier and the email address you share with us (which may be Apple's private relay address), along with your subscription entitlement and session credentials.
  • Synced memory content (optional): if you enable Memories Keeper sync, end-to-end encrypted copies of your memory text, details, and voice memos are stored on our servers. They are encrypted on your device before upload with keys that never leave your Apple account, so we cannot read them. Photos and videos are not synced to our servers.
  • Legacy delivery data (optional): if you arm legacy delivery, we store your recipient's email address, an encrypted legacy package sealed to your recipient's key (which we cannot open), your chosen delivery schedule or inactivity period, and app check-in timestamps used to detect inactivity.
  • Subscription and purchase status: entitlement and transaction state used to unlock paid features.
  • Device diagnostics (if enabled): crash or performance diagnostics provided by Apple.

Data Security

  • Local storage: Memory data is stored securely on your device.
  • Apple sync services: When available and enabled, data may be synchronized through Apple services, including iCloud, with protections provided by Apple. Note that Apple cloud data is not end-to-end encrypted for all content types.
  • Memories Keeper sync and legacy servers: When you opt in, our servers store memory content only in end-to-end encrypted form. Content is encrypted on your device (AES-256) before it is uploaded; sync keys are kept in your iCloud Keychain and legacy packages can only be opened by your chosen recipient. We hold ciphertext and cannot decrypt your memories.
  • Exports and sharing: When you export or share memories outside the app, the security of that data depends on the destination and method you choose.

How We Use Data

  • Memory text, photos, voice notes, and location: stored on-device to run the app and, if you enable sync, synchronized through Apple services, including iCloud/CloudKit, under your Apple account. If you create an account and enable Memories Keeper sync, memory text, details, and voice memos are also stored on our servers in end-to-end encrypted form that we cannot read, solely to sync your memories across your devices.
  • Account information: used to authenticate you, associate your synced data and subscription entitlement with your account, and operate the legacy delivery feature.
  • Contacts (if linked): used to support legacy contact and shared-memory features you explicitly configure.
  • Legacy settings and package details: if you enable the legacy feature, the selected recipient, package preferences, personal message, inactivity period, and reminder settings are used to manage reminders, display your settings, and help you prepare a legacy package. If you arm legacy delivery, the encrypted package, recipient email, and delivery schedule are stored on our servers so the package can be delivered to your recipient — either on the date you choose or after your chosen period of account inactivity. Only your recipient can decrypt the package.
  • Shared content: shared only when you take a sharing action in-app or through Apple's share sheet. Note that exported or shared data may leave Apple's ecosystem and is subject to the privacy practices of the sharing destination.
  • Subscription and purchase status: processed through StoreKit to validate, restore, and manage purchases.
  • Diagnostics: used to identify bugs, improve stability, and troubleshoot support issues.

Third-Party Services and Sharing

  • Apple iCloud/CloudKit: used only when you enable sync or other Apple-backed cloud features.
  • Apple StoreKit: used for subscriptions, in-app purchases, and purchase restoration.
  • Memories Keeper service (optional): if you create an account, we operate our own server for cross-device sync and legacy delivery. It stores account information and end-to-end encrypted content only — we cannot read your memories. The service is hosted on Render, a cloud infrastructure provider that processes data on our behalf.
  • Sign in with Apple: account creation and sign-in use Apple's authentication service; we never see your Apple ID password.
  • Website analytics: our website (memories-keeper.escapeplan.ae) uses Google Analytics to measure page visits, which sets cookies and shares usage data with Google under Google's privacy policy. This applies to the website only — the app itself contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No advertising SDKs: we do not integrate third-party ad networks for behavioral advertising.
  • No data sale: we do not sell personal data from memories or related account data.
  • Sharing destinations: when you share or export memories, the data may be transmitted to third-party services or apps you select. We do not control the privacy practices of these destinations.

Retention and Deletion

  • Memory data: kept until you delete specific memories or remove the app data. When you delete a memory, it is moved to "Recently Deleted" and retained for 30 days so you can restore it if needed. After 30 days the data is permanently erased from your device and, if iCloud sync is enabled, from your iCloud account.
  • Apple cloud data: retained in your Apple account until you delete memories in-app or delete app data from the relevant Apple cloud settings.
  • Memories Keeper server data: encrypted synced memories are updated and removed through the same sync process as you edit and delete memories. An armed legacy package is removed when you disarm legacy delivery. Deleting your account in-app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) removes your account information, synced data, and any undelivered legacy package from our servers; memories on your device and in iCloud are kept.
  • Purchase records: billing records are retained by Apple under Apple's legal and accounting obligations.
  • Diagnostics: retained only as long as needed for debugging and service reliability.
  • How to delete data: you can delete memories inside the app, remove cloud data from iOS iCloud settings, delete your account (and its server data) inside the app, and request account-related deletion via email.

User Rights and Requests

You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by emailing support@escapeplan.ae. We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete verified requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law.

International Transfers and Jurisdiction

Your data may be processed in jurisdictions where Apple and its infrastructure operate. If you use the optional Memories Keeper account features, account information and end-to-end encrypted content are processed on infrastructure operated by Render, which may be located in the United States. We rely on Apple's and our hosting provider's platform safeguards and applicable legal mechanisms for cross-border processing. This policy is governed by applicable laws of the jurisdiction of Escape Plan Developments FCZO, unless local law requires otherwise.

GDPR — Rights for Users in the European Economic Area

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or applicable local law:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of your personal data where there is no compelling reason for its continued processing.
  • Portability: request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction: request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

Our lawful basis for processing is performance of a contract (providing the app features you use) and legitimate interests (improving stability and security). We do not process special category data. To exercise any of these rights, email support@escapeplan.ae. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

CCPA — Rights for California Residents

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you the following rights:

  • Right to know: request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to delete: request deletion of personal information we have collected, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale: we do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To submit a CCPA request, email support@escapeplan.ae with "CCPA Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days.

Children's privacy

Memories Keeper is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
